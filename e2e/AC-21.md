# AC-21 – MVP acceptance checklist

AC-21 (SRS 17, SDD 7.3 and 8.4) asks that all 8 screens (UI-01 to UI-08) and the flows of UC-01 to UC-11 can be clicked through, per role, on the MVP build (`VITE_USE_MOCKS=true`). Nothing is saved: a reload resets the data.

The suite runs with `pnpm e2e` in Chrome, Firefox and Edge at 1366×768. The three journeys in `tests/ac21.spec.ts` click through every screen of each role with the menu only. The other spec files check each flow in detail.
